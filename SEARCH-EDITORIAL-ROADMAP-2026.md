## Publication requirements
Every article needs a substantive original explanation, descriptive title/meta, author and reviewer credit where appropriate, publication/update date, credible primary sources, internal links to hub/related articles, mobile readability, disclosures, and relevant CTAs. No invented case studies or insurer guarantees. Estate/legal content must be reviewed by a qualified attorney before publication; insurance marketing content should receive applicable compliance review.

## Traffic measurement
Measure Google Search Console queries, impressions, click-through rates and indexing; GA4 landing-page sessions, engaged sessions, CTA intents and form starts; reconcile real submissions with Netlify Forms. Publish a small initial cohort before expanding. Do not claim any keyword is high volume without measured data.

## Editorial launch gate
- Search Console and GA4 baseline established.
- Drafts reviewed for accuracy and regulation.
- Page routes live, indexable, added to sitemap, with canonical URLs.
- Internal navigation and inquiry paths work end-to-end.
- Publishing cadence sustainable (start with two substantive articles per month).
