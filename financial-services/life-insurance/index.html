Policy fundamentals

Different policies meet different needs

Life insurance can provide a benefit when a covered insured dies while the contract is in force. Costs, guarantees, potential cash value, underwriting and risk of lapse differ by policy.

  • Term life generally provides protection for a specified period.
  • Whole life typically uses contract-defined provisions for permanent coverage and cash value.
  • Universal life can provide flexible premiums with ongoing insurance charges and lapse risk.
  • Indexed universal life credits interest according to an index-linked formula, not by directly investing policy value in the stock index.
