YOUR CHOSEN TOPIC · LIFE INSURANCE

What if someone depends on your income or care?

Life insurance can help designated beneficiaries manage the financial impact of an insured person's death while coverage is in force, subject to the policy terms.

Who might explore this?

People with dependents, shared financial obligations, or others who would face costs or income loss after their death.

Ask yourself

If your contribution stopped unexpectedly, which ongoing expenses might other people need to cover?

What people often overlook

Three different needs. Three different questions.

Knowing the distinction helps you ask more informed questions and compare relevant approaches.

01 · LIFE INSURANCE

What happens financially if someone dies?

Who may want to learn more: parents, caregivers, partners, people supporting relatives and certain business owners or borrowers.

Why it matters: beneficiaries may need resources for living costs, outstanding obligations or financial transitions if a covered insured dies.

Consider: Who would depend on financial support, and for how long?

Read the Life Insurance Guide →
02 · LONG-TERM CARE

What if someone needs help for an extended period?

Who may want to learn more: adults planning for possible extended care, their families, and people concerned about caregiver responsibilities.

Why it matters: qualifying care may involve ongoing expenses and family responsibilities that medical insurance or Medicare may not fully address.

Consider: Who would help arrange or pay for care, and in what setting?

Explore the LTC Cost & Benefit-Gap Tool →
03 · ANNUITIES

How might retirement income need to work over time?

Who may want to learn more: people with long-term retirement or income-planning goals who can examine liquidity limits, costs and contract guarantees.

Why it matters: different annuity contracts may provide future income or tax-deferred accumulation but can impose fees, withdrawal restrictions and market risks.

Consider: When might you need access to the funds, and what income goal matters?
