Whole Life Insurance

Explore long-term death-benefit protection, premium commitments, cash value and dividends without confusing an illustration with a guarantee.

Explore the essentials

Coverage designed for the long term.

Whole life insurance is a type of permanent life insurance. It generally provides a death benefit while coverage remains in force and builds cash value according to the contract. Premium schedules and guarantees depend on the product design.

  • Review how many premiums are due and for how long.
  • Understand guaranteed cash values separately from any non-guaranteed dividends.
  • Consider cash value loans, loan interest and potential effects on benefits and taxes.
  • Examine surrender terms and whether the funding plan is sustainable.

Understand the mechanics

Three elements work together.

The policy's death benefit, contractual cash value and cost of coverage are related but not interchangeable.

01 · PROTECTION

Death benefit

Payable under the policy terms when a covered death occurs while the policy remains in force.

02 · VALUE

Cash value

Policy-defined accumulation and surrender values change over time.

03 · FUNDING

Premiums

Higher initial commitment than comparable term coverage is common; schedules vary by design.

04 · OPTIONAL

Dividends

Participating policies may pay dividends, but dividends are not guaranteed.

Read the contract carefully

Guaranteed is not the same as illustrated.

A professional comparison should examine the policy's required premiums, guarantees, dividends, surrender values, and loan provisions.

Guaranteed cash values

Review the contractual guaranteed value schedule rather than assuming an illustration will occur.

Dividends

Participating policies may declare dividends, but future amounts depend on insurer experience.

Policy loans

Loans accrue interest and may reduce benefits; failure to manage debt can create lapse or tax consequences.

Surrender considerations

Ending a contract may produce a surrender value different from total premiums paid and may have tax implications.

Frequently asked

Questions worth exploring

Use these before deciding whether this type of coverage fits a particular purpose.

Does whole life guarantee a return on every premium dollar?

No. Whole life has certain contractual guaranteed values, but it should not be treated as a universally guaranteed investment return.

Can I withdraw cash value?

Contractual loans or withdrawals may be possible. A loan or withdrawal can reduce benefits and may cause lapse or tax consequences.

Are dividends guaranteed?

No. Participating whole-life dividends are generally non-guaranteed.

Is whole life always better than term life?

No. The two products address different time horizons, budgets and needs. Suitability depends on the individual situation.
