Five Leadership Levels

The DominionStar progression.

Titles and promotions must be confirmed through the applicable company system. The platform records and celebrates verified progress.

1
TA

Training Associate

Entry stage focused on onboarding, licensing completion where applicable, foundational training, field development, and professional habits.

2

Associate

Developing production consistency, client-service competence, business-building discipline, and early mentoring habits.

3
SA

Senior Associate

Growing personal performance, strengthening recruiting and training capability, and helping newer associates progress.

4
MD

Marketing Director

Leading a developing organization, coaching team members, creating duplication, and building responsible leadership systems.

5
SMD

Senior Marketing Director

The leadership goal: building durable teams, developing other leaders, expanding impact, and protecting the DominionStar standard.

What Promotions Should Represent

More than a title.

Professional Competence

Licensing, compliance, training, client communication, documentation, and approved company systems.

Service & Responsibility

Helping families appropriately, operating ethically, following supervision, and protecting trust.

Leadership Duplication

Recruiting, coaching, developing other professionals, building systems, and helping future leaders advance.
