Understand the need

Care can affect the whole family

Long-term care planning goes beyond a nursing-home question. It may involve support at home, family caregivers, assisted living or other qualifying settings. Insurance benefits depend on contract terms and triggers.

  • What assistance might be needed for bathing, dressing, eating and other daily activities?
  • Could cognitive impairment affect the type of support needed?
  • Who might provide care, and which care settings should be considered?
  • What costs might a family need to fund through savings or qualifying coverage?

Common questions

Explore more before the call

These concepts are only a starting point; policy specifics require review.

Does Medicare pay for all long-term care?

Medicare generally does not cover long-term custodial care. Coverage of certain medically necessary skilled services is limited and subject to conditions.

Are long-term care and chronic-illness riders the same?

Not necessarily. A rider accelerating a life policy's death benefit may have different qualification standards, limits and effects on the remaining death benefit.

Can I get an exact quote from Aurora?

No. Aurora does not have live carrier pricing, underwriting or policy-specific illustrations. An authorized professional should confirm availability and terms.
