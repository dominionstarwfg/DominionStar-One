Professional foundations

A license is a process—not a shortcut

Insurance authority is administered by regulators. Requirements differ by state, province and insurance line. Consider this a planning guide, not an official regulator checklist.

  • Research resident state or provincial licensing rules from the regulator.
  • Use authorized education and examination providers where required.
  • Complete applicable background screening and the license application.
  • Confirm carrier appointments, product training, compliance and renewal requirements.

Common questions

Useful answers before scheduling

Expand each question to learn more.

Do requirements vary by state?

Yes. Fees, fingerprints, training and continuing education requirements differ. Verify official regulator instructions before taking action.

Will passing an exam mean I can immediately sell insurance?

Not necessarily. Licensing, contracting, appointments, product training and supervision may still be required.
