Education & College Planning

What deserves your attention?

Educational starting points to help you prepare for an informed conversation.

Start with a goal

Consider the student's timeline, education choices, possible inflation and what your family can realistically set aside.

Know the alternatives

Savings plans, scholarships, grants, cash flow and responsible borrowing can contribute to an education strategy.

Review tax rules

529 plans and other savings vehicles have different eligibility, use and taxation requirements.

Balance competing priorities

Education planning should account for emergency reserves, insurance and retirement needs.

This information is general education, not a personalized product recommendation, tax opinion or investment advice. Availability and appropriate licensing vary by jurisdiction.
