Deeper learning

Professional growth is a practice

Passing an exam is not the same as being ready for every client conversation. Growth includes communication, compliance, understanding policy illustrations, knowing when to ask a supervisor and improving through feedback.

  • Develop clear explanations of financial concepts without overstating product benefits.
  • Learn basic fact-finding, suitability boundaries and ethical communication.
  • Practice documentation, follow-up and policyholder service habits.
  • Use available group sessions and one-on-one mentorship when offered.

Questions, answered

Explore before you book

Open the questions to understand how the pathway works.

Are there regular mentorship sessions?

DominionStar offers one-on-one and group mentorship pathways, subject to scheduling and availability. Request specific session details from the team.

Can licensed agents participate?

The pathway is designed to be relevant to developing professionals, including those already licensed. Eligibility and availability are confirmed individually.

Will mentorship qualify me to sell every insurance product?

No. Additional carrier, product, appointment and jurisdiction-specific requirements may apply.
