Deeper learning

Leadership is measured in people

A successful leader must be able to communicate expectations, help others prepare responsibly, give feedback, respect compliance limits and promote realistic professional goals.

  • Model reliable professional habits, including documentation and follow-through.
  • Coach developing agents without promising compensation or promotion.
  • Understand team-training responsibilities and escalation paths.
  • Develop systems for supporting people across different jurisdictions.
