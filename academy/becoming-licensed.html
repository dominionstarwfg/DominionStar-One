How becoming licensed generally works

Insurance licensing is regulated by the state or province where a professional intends to conduct business. The exact process varies, but it commonly includes education, an examination, an application, background review, and company appointments.

Typical stages

  1. Review local requirements.
  2. Complete pre-licensing study.
  3. Pass the applicable exam.
  4. Submit the application and disclosures.
  5. Complete contracting and appointments.
  6. Maintain continuing education and renewals.
Verify current requirements with the appropriate regulator.
