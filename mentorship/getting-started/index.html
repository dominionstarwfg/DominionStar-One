Questions, answered

Explore before you book

Open the questions to understand how the pathway works.

Will DominionStar license me directly?

No. State or provincial regulators determine whether an individual qualifies. DominionStar can explain the general process and available mentoring support.

Do I need to be licensed before asking questions?

No. Exploratory conversations can occur before licensing, but activity requiring licensure cannot begin until proper authorization is obtained.

Is there an income guarantee?

No. Financial-services opportunities can involve expenses and variable compensation. Earnings and career results are not guaranteed.
