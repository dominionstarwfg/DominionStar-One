DominionStar Knowledge Center

Recruiting is the beginning—not the end of the support.

See how DominionStar guides recruits through course selection, licensing, onboarding, Zoom training, client readiness, business building, and leadership.

DominionStar's operating principle is simple: recruits should not be expected to figure out the entire journey alone.

Before the exam

Support may include explaining the opportunity, identifying approved pre-licensing education, reviewing study expectations, and helping the recruit understand jurisdiction-specific requirements.

After the exam

DominionStar helps recruits identify fingerprinting or background-review requirements, navigate the application process, and understand resident or non-resident licensing where appropriate.

After licensing

Training can occur one-on-one or through Zoom. The focus includes onboarding, compliance, company systems, products, illustrations, communication, prospecting, client readiness, business development, and leadership.

Long-term development

The journey continues through service, professional growth, recruiting, mentorship, and developing future leaders responsibly.

Important: This article is general educational information. Requirements change and must be verified with the applicable regulator, approved education provider, carrier, and platform at the time you proceed.
