Why life insurance matters

Life insurance may provide a death benefit for eligible covered claims, helping beneficiaries address living expenses, debt or future goals.

Term vs. permanent coverage

Term life commonly covers a set period. Permanent policies may have cash-value provisions and different long-term costs and risks.

Beneficiary planning

Review beneficiaries and ownership arrangements periodically, particularly after major life changes.

Your next step

Explore needs, coverage amounts, affordability and policy-specific guarantees with an appropriately licensed professional.
