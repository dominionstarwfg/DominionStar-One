Privacy

Privacy Policy

How information supplied to DominionStar through public inquiries is used.

Information and privacy

Your information matters.

Information we may collect

Contact details and information you voluntarily submit, such as name, email, phone, location, interests, scheduling preferences and questions.

How it is used

To respond to inquiries, schedule conversations, provide requested information, improve our website, and understand marketing performance.

Form and analytics service providers

Website submissions may be processed through hosting and form providers such as Netlify. Google Analytics may process browser and usage data under its applicable terms. We measure general website actions, such as visiting inquiry pages, clicking contact links, beginning an inquiry and reaching the form confirmation page. We do not intentionally send inquiry form answers to analytics.

Campaign attribution

If you arrive through a link with marketing campaign tags (source, medium, campaign, or content), the website may remember those labels and your first page visited within the current browser tab session. These labels may accompany an inquiry you voluntarily submit so we can evaluate which outreach led to your request. Campaign tags should never include sensitive or personal information.

Your choices

To request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, contact DominionStar at DominionStarwfg@gmail.com.

Contact

Email DominionStarwfg@gmail.com or call (346) 204-2641 for general questions.

This public policy is a general overview, not a substitute for a legal and compliance review of actual data processing and retention practices.
