Seven Stages

From first conversation to leadership.

The sequence may vary by jurisdiction, provider, licensing status, and individual readiness.

1

Discover

Understand DominionStar, the independent opportunity, expectations, and disclosures before deciding.

  • Opportunity conversation
  • Career assessment
  • Questions about schedule, compensation, and licensing
  • No-pressure decision
2

Prepare for Licensing

Choose an approved pre-licensing provider for the applicable jurisdiction and create a study plan.

  • Approved course-provider verification
  • XCEL Solutions may be available with a DominionStar-related discount
  • Other approved providers, such as Kaplan, may also be considered
  • Study support and accountability
3

Pass the Exam & Apply

After passing, DominionStar helps identify the next required steps rather than leaving the recruit alone.

  • Fingerprinting where required
  • Resident license application
  • Non-resident licensing guidance where appropriate
  • Regulator and jurisdiction verification
4

Complete Onboarding & Compliance

Complete required setup before regulated activity or compensation.

  • Background-review and onboarding steps
  • Errors & Omissions coverage where required
  • Annual Anti-Money Laundering training where applicable
  • Platform access, contracting, and appointments
5

Learn the Business

Training begins before independent client activity. Delivery may be one-on-one or by Zoom.

  • Company portals and systems
  • Product education
  • Illustration software
  • Client communication and documentation
  • Compliance-first practices
6

Serve Families & Build

Begin activity only after licensing, appointments, training, and readiness requirements are satisfied.

  • Prospecting and referrals
  • Supervised field development
  • Helping families through approved solutions
  • Building a responsible business
7

Develop Leaders

Experienced professionals learn to recruit, mentor, train, and guide others through the same journey.

  • Leadership development
  • Team support
  • Training others responsibly
  • Long-term professional growth
Important: Licensing, fingerprinting, fees, provider approval, background checks, E&O, AML, appointments, products, and contracting vary by jurisdiction and provider. Exact requirements must be verified when a person proceeds.
