Canada licensing education

Understand the licensing pathway.

Insurance licensing is administered by provincial or territorial regulators. These are general reference points, not a promise of licensing or contracting.

STEP 1

Choose your province

Identify where you intend to seek a license and consult that regulator.

STEP 2

Review LLQP or other required education

In many jurisdictions, approved Life Licence Qualification Program training and exams are part of the process.

STEP 3

Meet regulator requirements

Applications may involve sponsorship, E&O coverage, suitability and other provincial criteria.

STEP 4

Confirm contracting and practice authority

Carrier appointments, training and applicable authorization must be confirmed before regulated activities.

Canada is not one single insurance licensing jurisdiction. Requirements in Québec differ in important respects, including AMF certification and ethics requirements. U.S. insurance licenses do not automatically authorize Canadian insurance activity.

Canada recruiting FAQ

Your questions, answered.

You can read these details before requesting a career conversation.

Can Canadians join DominionStar?

Canadian recruiting and mentorship inquiries are welcome. Actual affiliation, contracting and licensed activity depend on provincial rules, insurer/platform authorization and program availability.

Does a U.S. life insurance license work in Canada?

Not automatically. The relevant provincial or territorial regulator and company rules determine authorization.

Do you offer mentorship online?

DominionStar's learning approach includes remote one-on-one and group sessions. Confirm which programs, mentors and schedules are currently available for Canadian prospects.

Do I need to be licensed before contacting you?

No. You can request information before licensing. However, regulatory activities cannot begin until the required permissions and approvals are in place.

Will I earn money after signing up?

No income, client volume, licensing outcome or promotion can be guaranteed. Ask about costs, compensation conditions and eligibility before making a decision.
